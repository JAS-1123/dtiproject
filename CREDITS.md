## Pride and Prejudice — Chapter I excerpt

- **Author:** Jane Austen
- **Original work:** *Pride and Prejudice* (first published 1813; public domain)
- **Text transcription used:** [Wikisource, *Pride and Prejudice (1817)/Chapter 1*](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Pride_and_Prejudice_(1817)/Chapter_1)
- **Source-page terms:** The Wikisource page states that its text is available under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) license. See the license notice at the linked page.
- **Changes for this prototype:** The chapter excerpt is divided into eight timestamped passages; punctuation and some archaic spellings are normalized for speech synthesis and retrieval.
- **Audio:** `frontend/public/audio/pride_and_prejudice_chapter1.wav` is a new synthetic narration generated with eSpeak from the excerpt. It is not copied from an existing audiobook or actor's recording.

This project includes only Chapter I as a presentation sample, not the full novel. The project README links the text source and describes the synthetic audio. Check the source-page license and local law before reusing the excerpt or derived audio in another context.
